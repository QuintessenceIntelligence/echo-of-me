## 1. Purpose

This register lists every source of Echo of Me project knowledge. For each one it records origin, authenticity, authority and the unique content that the consolidation recovered from it. Each source has a stable ID (`S-xx` for sources present in the Project, `M-xx` for referenced artifacts that are missing). Canonical documents cite these IDs, for example `[S-05 §2.6]`.

## 4. Unique content recovered per source

Each list shows only content that appears in that source and nowhere else (or that only that source states authoritatively). Everything listed has been carried into the canonical set. The destination is shown in brackets.

### S-01 Scope & Brief (original, 2025)
- Exact six-phase timeline: UX/UI 2 wk, Core Dev 4, AI Engine 3, **Audio & Voice AI 2**, **Private Pilot 2**, Launch Prep 1, which **sums to 14 weeks** [01B §2.6, R6 C-04].
- Itemised cost lines that sum exactly to US$20,500 (minima) and US$30,000 (maxima). The "optional" voice line (US$2,000–3,000) is **included** in the total [01B §2.6].
- The five original core goals, including "Leverage collective insights across families to enhance emotional depth" [01A §3, R2 DR-021].
- Original module wording: "Lightweight GPT-powered engine trained on parent data"; "Chat interface for child to 'talk' to parent's conscience"; "Community-sourced wisdom and trending parent topics"; "Dashboard for parent to manage content and age-based access" [01C].
- Original vision sentence ("a timeless, evolving version of themselves — a voice, a conscience, and a guide…") [01A §2].
- "respond to their children's questions **in their voice and tone**" [R6 C-13].
- Research summary: YOV judged "most closely" aligned; reference to "grief-tech applications" [01B §3.1].

### S-02 Full Project Dossier (original, 2025)
- Market framing: "digitally aware parents, especially those concerned with legacy, estate planning, or terminal care" [01B §3.4].
- Five original differentiators: **longitudinal parent-AI training; event-triggered messages; community-informed prompts; child age-gating & ethical constraints; voice + personality continuity** [01A §5, 01C M10].
- **Original ethics mechanisms: "opt-in training, transparency logs, and manual override of AI replies"** [R2 DR-030, 01C M06].
- Vault scope in the original MVP was **text/audio/video** entries [R6 C-06].
- Alternative five-phase timeline: Design 2, Development 4, AI/Voice 3, **Testing** 2, Launch Prep 1, which **sums to 12 weeks**. This explains the "12–14 weeks" range [R6 C-04].
- Competitor specifics: YOV "Versonas" trained on emails, texts and voice; Eternime "did not launch fully"; Lifenaut "nonprofit" (and, inconsistently, "open source") [01B §3.1, R6 C-21].
- Strategic narrative ("Parents often leave this world with wisdom unspoken…"; "A companion when they need it most") [01A §8, R6 C-22].
- Reference list: HereAfter.ai, StoryFile, YOV, Replika, Lifenaut, Eternime, SafeBeyond, **MIT Augmented Eternity**; inspirations: Microsoft, OpenAI, **Terasem Movement** [01B §3.1].
