## 1. Summary
- The original 2025 research report is **missing**. On 16 Nov 2026 the founder confirmed that S-03 was regenerated in ChatGPT on that date because the original could not be retrieved, and that the original sat in `Echo_of_Me_Project_Documents.zip` alongside S-01 and S-02.
- **S-03 is a provisional secondary reference.** It is retained and labelled as a reconstruction. Its content is **not historical fact** unless independently corroborated (§4).
- Of S-03's **31 claim groups**, **13 (about 40%) are corroborated by the 2025 originals**. The rest are recovered fragments (2), reconstructed (5: traceable only to other 2026 reconstructions), inferred (8: 2026 analysis written in a historical voice), mixed (2) or founder-confirmed provenance (1).
- **Circularity warning.** S-03 says it was "assembled from the later Echo of Me handoff" (S-06). Agreement between S-03 and S-06 is therefore **not** independent corroboration. Only S-01, S-02, S-17 and founder statements count as independent historical evidence.
- **Most promising recovery path:** the June 2025 ChatGPT conversation. The surviving originals were produced by ChatGPT's `python-docx` code tool, so the conversation probably contains the script that generated the report, with its full text (§7).
- Nothing in this register blocks architecture, requirements or MVP work (DR-048). It affects the **historical record**, any **"our 2025 research found…"** claims, and the **origin of competitor findings**.

## 5. Content in the originals that S-03 lacks (gaps in the reconstruction)
These are **historical evidence (HG-1)** that the reconstruction dropped. They are now preserved in the canonical set.
1. "**Grief-tech** applications" as part of the research scope (S-01 §8).
2. YOV "Versonas" **trained on emails, texts and voice data** to emulate a person posthumously; "closest match" (S-02).

Comparative judgments (S-02 §4): HereAfter "high realism via voice, limited to pre-recorded"; StoryFile "no generative capability"; Eternime "strong conceptual match, poor execution"; Replika "generative but not legacy-based"; Lifenaut "open source and early stage"; SafeBeyond "great structure… but static".
4. References: HereAfter.ai, StoryFile, YOV, Replika, Lifenaut, Eternime, SafeBeyond, **MIT Augmented Eternity**; inspirations **Microsoft, OpenAI, Terasem Movement** (S-02 §10).
5. Differentiators: **event-triggered messages**, **voice + personality continuity**, community-informed prompts, longitudinal training, child age-gating (S-02 §5).
6. Ethics mechanisms: **opt-in training, transparency logs, manual override of AI replies** (S-02 §6).
7. Market: "digitally aware parents… **legacy, estate planning, or terminal care**" (S-02 §2).
8. Voice ambition: answers "**in their voice and tone**"; "ElevenLabs or similar for **voice cloning**" (S-01 §1; S-02 §6).

The handoffs also contain items absent from S-03: Eter9, LifeChronicles / ethical wills, the Microsoft chatbot patent, and the Reuters/ABC citations (all HG-4). Whether the original report covered them is **unknown**.
