## 1. Purpose of this charter
Historical claims carry evidence grades (R8 §2): **HG-1** original 2025 evidence · **HG-2** founder-confirmed · **HG-3** recovered fragment · **HG-4** reconstructed · **HG-5** inferred. Charter *principles* are current founder policy, not historical claims.

This charter fixes the mission, the non-negotiable principles and the boundaries that every specification, design, line of code and piece of marketing must respect. Where any later document conflicts with this charter, the charter prevails until it is formally amended.

## 2. Founding idea and vision

**Founding phrase (kept verbatim):** the founder described an application that uses AI, taught gradually by its creator, as a **"copy of my conscience"** that his children could consult if he died. The concept was initiated on **29 May 2025** [S-06 §3.1; *reconstructed date, HG-4*]. The name **Echo of Me** was in use in the original documents generated on **12 June 2025** [S-01, S-02; HG-1, Drive file dates].

**Vision statement (original, 2025)** [S-01 §3; HG-1]:
> "To create an AI-enhanced legacy platform that allows every parent to leave behind a timeless, evolving version of themselves — a voice, a conscience, and a guide — that speaks lovingly and truthfully to their children when they need it most."

**Charter interpretation (CONFIRMED by S-08):** "a timeless, evolving version of themselves" is read as *an evolving, faithfully governed body of the parent's own recorded memories, values, reasoning and voice*. It is **not** a claim that consciousness can be preserved or that the AI *is* the parent.

**Mission:** Help parents progressively preserve their authentic memories, values, reasoning, wisdom and love, so that the people they choose can find and understand them, faithfully and safely, when it matters most.

**Governing principle** [S-06 §5.10]: *Success is not measured by how convincingly AI pretends to be a person, but by how faithfully, meaningfully and responsibly it makes that person's genuine knowledge available across time.*

## 3. The problem

- Parents run out of time to say everything. Children do not yet know which questions to ask, and important wisdom goes unsaid: the pain of **"unasked questions"** and **"words left unsaid"** [S-02 §2; S-06 §1.1].
- Albums, journals, recordings, memorial sites and scheduled messages preserve *artefacts*, but they rely on the recipient knowing where to look [S-03 §2; S-05 §1.2].
- A parent's value lies not only in *what* they experienced but in *how they thought, why they decided, what they believed and learned* [S-05 §1.1].
- Illustrative future questions [S-05 §1.2]: "How did you know when you were making the right decision?" · "What would you have told me about my first heartbreak?" · "What did you believe about forgiveness?" · "Why did you make that difficult career decision?" · "What were you most afraid of when you became a parent?" · "What would you want me to know before I become a parent myself?"

**Original core goals (2025)** [S-01 §3], preserved with the charter interpretation:

| Original goal | Charter interpretation |
|---|---|
| Build an AI that learns from a parent's words, values and stories | An AI interface **grounded** in the parent's approved words, values and stories (method PROPOSED: DR-022) |
| Offer parents a secure, structured platform to train and curate their conscience | Guided, secure, structured capture and curation (CONFIRMED) |
| Enable children to ask questions and receive guidance long after the parent is gone | CONFIRMED CONCEPT; posthumous release subject to G3 |
| Leverage collective insights across families to enhance emotional depth | Only as opt-in, de-identified, aggregate *prompt themes*; never personal content (DR-021, PROPOSED) |
| Preserve family voice, memory and identity across generations | CONFIRMED; synthetic voice subject to G6 |

## 4. Audience
- **Primary:** parents (and guardians) who want to leave something meaningful for their children (DR-004, DR-043).
- **Primary beneficiaries:** their children, across life stages, and potentially future generations.
- **Possible later expansion:** partners, grandparents, mentors, other loved ones, wider personal or professional legacy [S-04 §1.1].
- **Historical segment hypotheses (unvalidated)** [S-02 §2; S-04 §3.4]: digitally aware parents; parents aged 28–50; estate-planning-oriented families; terminally ill parents; expatriate or military families; referral partners. Sensitive groups (terminally ill, bereaved) are **not** default early cohorts (DR-025).

## 5. What Echo of Me preserves and does

**Five conceptual capabilities** [S-05 §1.3; HG-4]: **Preservation** (memories, voice, values, stories, feelings, advice) · **Interpretation** (organise into contextual, searchable knowledge) · **Interaction** (authorised recipients ask questions and receive evidence-grounded responses) · **Personalisation** (presentation suited to age, circumstance and consented data) · **Continuity** (access over time, at milestones, and potentially after death).

**Ten dimensions of the "digital conscience"** [S-06 §1.3; HG-4/HG-5, a useful design model]:

| Dimension | Captured material |
|---|---|
| Identity | Biography, family relationships, defining experiences |
| Personality | Communication style, humour, temperament, recurring expressions |
| Values | Integrity, kindness, courage, forgiveness and other principles |
| Reasoning | How choices, risks, dilemmas and competing values were weighed |
| Emotional wisdom | Love, disappointment, anxiety, grief, resilience |
| Practical knowledge | Career, learning, money, relationships, ordinary decisions |
| Life lessons | Successes, failures, regrets and lessons learned |
| Directed messages | Advice, stories and loving messages for specific recipients |
| Voice | Original speaking style; potentially authorised synthesised speech (G6) |
| Evolving knowledge | New memories, changed beliefs, corrections, later reflections |

**Original 2025 differentiators** [S-02 §5; HG-1], with their current homes: longitudinal parent-AI "training" → progressive capture (02, 05) · event-triggered messages → Module 10 · community-informed prompts → Module 05 within DR-021 · child age-gating and ethical constraints → Module 06 · voice and personality continuity → original audio now, synthetic voice under G6.

**Differentiation hypothesis (unproven; HG-5 framing, not a 2025 finding)** [S-03 §5; S-04 §3.2; S-06 §3.3]: parent-guided capture of values *and reasoning*, plus context-aware, source-cited retrieval, plus strong author control and recipient protection. The market is not empty, and this must be proven at the job-to-be-done level.

**Full module catalogue** (DR-011): 01 Parent & Family Profiles · 02 Memory & Wisdom Vault · 03 AI Conscience Engine · 04 Interaction Simulator · 05 Guided Prompt Library · 06 Dashboard, Permissions & Safeguards · 07 Feedback Loop · 08 Memory Canvas · 08A Playful Identity Image Generator · 09 Video Canvas / The Echo Edit · 10 Timed & Milestone Messages. See 01C.

## 6. Roles and stakeholders
| Role | Charter definition | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Author** (parent, legacy creator) | Owns the source material; records, approves, sets recipients, access and release rules | S-04; S-05; S-06 |
| **Recipient** (child, beneficiary) | Consults authorised, age-appropriate material; has their own data rights | S-04; S-05; S-06 |
| **Guardian** | Safeguards a minor recipient's access; exact powers UNRESOLVED | S-05 §1.4; S-06 |
| **Trusted Contact / Custodian** | Nominated by the author to help verify release events; **no unilateral override by default**; exact powers UNRESOLVED | S-04 `TrustedContact`; S-06 |
| **Contributor** (family/friend) | May add recollections in a **separately labelled** layer; never converted into "what the author believed" (DR-045, PROPOSED) | S-06 §1.4, §4.11 |
| **Platform Administrator** | Operates, secures and moderates; **no unbounded access** to private family content | S-05; S-06 |

## 8. Non-negotiable principles

| # | Principle | Meaning in practice |
|---|---|---|
| **P-01** | **Authentic, not alive** | Echo of Me preserves recorded memories, expressions, lessons and values. It never claims to preserve consciousness, never markets itself as the parent being alive, and never acts as a replacement parent. |
| **P-02** | **Never fabricate** | No invented autobiographical memories; no opinion attributed to the author without supporting evidence; no predictions dressed as the author's view. |
| **P-03** | **Provenance and uncertainty are visible** | Every answer shows whether it is a direct recording or quote, a grounded interpretation, or not recorded, with source references. Conflicts and changed views are disclosed, not smoothed over. |
| **P-04** | **AI identity is always disclosed** | Recipients always know they are using an AI interface to recorded material, including when they ask "are you my mom/dad?". |
| **P-05** | **Author consent and control** | Consent is purpose-specific, versioned and revocable. The author approves what enters the knowledge base, who sees what, and when. |
| **P-06** | **Protect children and the grieving** | Adults first. Age policy is enforced by the system. Child-facing features are non-diagnostic, with no covert profiling. Interactions are grief-informed and do not maximise engagement. Crisis situations are routed to human help. |
| **P-07** | **Policy before retrieval** | Identity, relationship, age, release and sensitivity rules are enforced by the application **before** any content reaches a model, never by a prompt alone. |
| **P-08** | **Privacy by default** | Family data is highly sensitive and isolated per legacy. No cross-family use of personal content, and no training of anyone else's AI on it, unless the user gives meaningful explicit consent after a privacy review. |
| **P-09** | **Model and vendor portability** | Original sources are canonical and independent of any model, embedding format or vendor. Derived data can be regenerated. |
| **P-10** | **Durable and honest about permanence** | Design for decades: export, backups, continuity planning. Never promise "forever" beyond what is sustainably funded and legally possible. |
| **P-11** | **Rights-respecting voice, likeness and creativity** | Synthetic voice or likeness only with verified, explicit consent and labelling, including rules for after death. Creative modules use rights-cleared material only. Generated images are never presented as historical evidence. |
| **P-12** | **Evidence over claims** | No capability, privacy guarantee, number, user count or partnership is claimed without proof. No feature is "done" without test evidence. Mock or illustrative UI is never presented as working. |

## 11. What Echo of Me is not
Not a griefbot designed for engagement. Not a deepfake or voice-cloning service. Not a generic personality chatbot. Not a social-media afterlife. Not a clinical or psychological assessment tool. Not a guarantee of indefinite storage.
